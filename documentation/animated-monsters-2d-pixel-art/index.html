Quick start

  1. Import the package into your Unity project. The included demos were prepared and validated with Unity 6.3 LTS and URP 2D.
  2. Open Assets/CloudedStudio/Animated Monsters 2D Pixel Art/Prefabs.
  3. Drag a character prefab into your scene. Its SpriteRenderer and Animator are already connected to the character's sprites and controller.
  4. Press Play. The character starts in its default Idle or Flying Idle state.
  5. Use the character's clips and Animator states to connect animations to your own game logic.

Prefabs use the URP 2D Sprite-Lit-Default material. Include a 2D light in the scene. If using a different rendering setup, assign a compatible sprite material.

Animations

Clips are authored at 12 frames per second. The available actions vary by character: the collection includes Idle, locomotion, flying cycles, attacks, reactions, gestures, and death animations.

Continuous cycles have Loop Time enabled. One-off actions keep Loop Time disabled. The individual demo intentionally replays every selected action so it can be inspected repeatedly; it does not change the original clips' loop settings.

Controllers contain the corresponding animation states. Play a state through your Animator or add transitions that suit your project.

Pixel settings

  • Sprite Mode: Multiple.
  • Pixels Per Unit: 32.
  • Filter Mode: Point.
  • Pivot: Bottom Center.
  • Frame cells: 64 × 64 or 128 × 128, depending on the character.

Keep Point filtering and use integer enlargement to retain crisp pixels. Avoid resizing individual characters to fill the same area: their original proportions and pixel density should remain consistent.

Demo scenes

AllMonstersIdle

Shows all 46 characters together, in an eight-column grid, playing their default looping Idle animations. The pixel-perfect camera applies the same integer enlargement to the whole collection.

MonsterShowcase

Browse one character at a time with Previous / Next or the arrow keys. Click an animation button to inspect it. The viewer uses a fixed 4× enlargement and reserves space for the character's actions.

Use Individual in the gallery to open the character viewer, and View all to return. Include both scenes in your build's scene list to enable this navigation in a player build.

The browser demo starts in the complete gallery. A desktop browser or a landscape viewport is recommended for the complete interface.
